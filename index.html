Why JALEES ?

What is unique about JALEES that readers will be able to create rooms . Anyone who wants to start reading a book can open a reading room. Although the store is only intended for Jordanians, these rooms will be for readers from all over the world. They can discuss the events ,make predections and analysis ,arrange events or meeting in real life .And There will be an option to burn the events of the book if the reader wants. Rooms will consider people from all the world not only from Jordan. All of this is to create an enthusiastic environment for readers and encourage people to read.

Atomic Habits

"Atomic Habits" by James Clear offers practical strategies to build good habits and break bad ones through small, incremental changes. The book introduces the Four Laws of Behavior Change: make habits obvious, attractive, easy, and satisfying. It's based on scientific research and real-life examples.

Can't Hurt Me

"Can't Hurt Me" by David Goggins is a memoir and self-help book that chronicles Goggins' transformation from a troubled youth to a Navy SEAL and ultra-endurance athlete. Goggins shares his life's challenges and how he overcame them through mental toughness, self-discipline, and relentless hard work. The book offers insights and practical advice on pushing past limits and embracing discomfort to achieve personal growth and success.

Rich Dad Poor Dad

"Rich Dad Poor Dad" by Robert T. Kiyosaki is a personal finance book that contrasts the financial philosophies of Kiyosaki's two "dads"—his biological father (Poor Dad) and the father of his best friend (Rich Dad). The book emphasizes the importance of financial education, investing in assets, and entrepreneurship. Kiyosaki advocates for understanding the difference between assets and liabilities, building wealth through investments, and seeking financial independence rather than relying on traditional employment and saving strategies.
